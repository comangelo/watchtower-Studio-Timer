Quick guide

The Watchtower Study in 7 steps

ESCAEUGLENDEITFRPTPLNLRO

⚠ Important: always conduct with JW Library or the printed Watchtower. The app only helps you keep track of the time.

  1. 1

    Download the magazine

    In “This week’s article”, tap “1. Download the magazine from jw.org”. It is downloaded in the language of the app.

  2. 2

    Open it in the app

    Go back to the app and tap the green button “2. Choose the downloaded PDF”. Choose the file: the app finds this week’s article by itself.

  3. 3

    Check the end time

    Check what time the study ends. If needed, tap “Change end time”.

  4. 4

    Open presentation mode

    Tap the ⛶ button in the bottom bar. It is the recommended way to conduct: everything large and no distractions.

  5. 5

    Start the study

    When you begin, tap “Start study”. The introduction starts.

  6. 6

    During the study

    Tap “Next” as each paragraph begins and “Comment” each time someone comments. The colour tells you how you are doing: green, on time; yellow, start wrapping up; red, move on. The “Next” button turns red too, and pulses when there is no time left to make up.

  7. 7

    Finish and see the report

    After the review, the button takes you to the conclusion. When you are done, tap “Finish study” and then “View report” to see how long each part took. It is saved in the “Study history”.

The pictures use an invented sample article.
